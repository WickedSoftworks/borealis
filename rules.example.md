# Rules

This instance is for sharing files with people you work with. By uploading
or sharing a file here, you agree to these rules.

- **Only share what you have the right to share.** No pirated media or
  other people's private data.
- **No malware.** Uploads are scanned, and flagged files are withheld.
- **Links are temporary.** Set an end date that fits, and revoke links you
  no longer need.
- **Respect the space.** Every account has a quota; the trash counts toward
  it until it's emptied.

Questions or a takedown request: write to [admin@example.com](mailto:admin@example.com).
